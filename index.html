02 / PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Understand the need.
Shape the whole product.

I start by understanding what is happening, who it affects and what a better outcome would look like. Then I break the problem into smaller parts, compare possible solutions and work through the details with the people involved.

More about me
01

Understand before deciding

I ask questions, trace the existing workflow and look for the cause behind the symptoms. I clarify constraints and assumptions early so I’m solving the right problem.

02

Choose with trade-offs in mind

I compare approaches by their usefulness, complexity and effort. I discuss decisions with the team and prefer a clear, maintainable solution that fits the problem.

03

Deliver, observe and improve

I work in manageable steps, check important edge cases and communicate what is changing. After delivery, I use feedback and actual behaviour to see what needs improving.
