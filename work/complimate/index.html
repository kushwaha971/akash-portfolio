Overview

I worked on Complimate while employed at Onelab Ventures as a Software Development Engineer I. Complimate is a compliance management product bringing client records, documents, obligations and coordination into a shared workspace.

Product context

The product brings compliance-related records and coordination into a shared workspace. Its public offering spans client onboarding, documents, obligations and team tasks. My Onelab engagement focused on application delivery and integration, rather than ownership of the full product.

The Challenge

Compliance workflows require structured records, predictable access and clear task states. Interface and API behaviour must help users manage detailed information without losing context.

My contribution

01

Worked across the React.js frontend and Node.js backend, integrating application workflows with PostgreSQL through Sequelize and supporting AWS delivery.

02

Contributed to the Complimate client engagement at Onelab Ventures.

03

Worked on API-backed application workflows, validation and administrative functionality.

04

Collaborated with the delivery team on debugging, maintainability and production support.

Decisions & outcome

My focus was delivering and debugging application features within the Onelab team: integrating APIs, validating data, improving maintainability and supporting releases.
