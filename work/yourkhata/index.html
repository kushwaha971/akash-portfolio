Overview

I’m building YourKhata to keep related business information and everyday operations connected. I handle the product research, interface, Python/Django backend, PostgreSQL data model, AI integration and deployment myself. The product supports multiple tenants and multiple businesses, with clear access boundaries between their records.

The Challenge

The hard part is making records stay consistent across invoices, stock, payments and reports while keeping each business separate. AI adds another requirement: a useful response needs the correct business context, rather than an unrestricted view of all the stored information.

My contribution

01

Translate business workflows into a product that connects ledgers, invoicing, inventory, purchases, expenses and payments.

02

Build the backend in Python and Django REST Framework with PostgreSQL, and the web experience in Next.js, React and TypeScript.

03

Implement multi-tenant and multi-business access, with owner, admin, staff and accountant roles controlling access to records and operations.

04

Integrate LLM-powered assistance and RAG workflows to retrieve relevant business context and turn questions into useful responses grounded in that information.

05

Connect AI functionality to the application’s data and permissions so the selected business determines the context available to the assistant.

06

Build reports, reminders, traceable corrections, statements, import/export and payment-related workflows around the same business records.

07

Handle Docker-based deployment, scheduled jobs and production configuration, alongside debugging and iteration.

08

Implement SEO and GEO foundations: discoverable pages, metadata, canonicals, sitemaps, FAQs and structured data. Achieved PageSpeed 99+ on desktop and 90+ on mobile.

Decisions & outcome

I keep the accounting and record-management operations deterministic, while using LLM and RAG capabilities to help people find and understand information. Tenant and business permissions apply to the context supplied to AI as well as the ordinary application workflows.
